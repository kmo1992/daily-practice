# Journal

## 2026-01-25 - Atomic Habits and the morning stack

### Morning stack (current)

- Workout (burpees, Navy SEALs, or workout video)
- Pull-ups
- Mobility
- Coffee
- Reading

### Four-step habit loop mapping

- Cue: Habit tracker on the office computer and the morning setup in the same room.
- Craving: Anticipation of coffee + reading, and the identity boost of being a practitioner.
- Response: Complete workout, pull-ups, and mobility with guided videos and timers (no friction).
- Reward: Wipe off, make coffee, fill the Nalgene, sit down and read before work. Satisfaction from starting the day right.

### Four laws for making good habits stick

- Obvious: Tracker is visible on the main workstation used every day.
- Attractive: Coffee + reading paired as the reward, plus the "job well done" feeling.
- Easy: No commute, no decision fatigue, videos tell me what to do, home setup is ready.
- Satisfying: Clear end-of-stack ritual (coffee + reading) and identity reinforcement.

### Other habits and constraints

- Sleep: Exercise makes sleep easier and more consistent.
- Water: Three Nalgenes, front-loaded earlier in the day to avoid waking up at night.
- Nutrition: Starts with points, which creates early momentum. Still need a stronger system here.

### App ideas to support this system

- Add a "habit stack" view to show chains (Workout -> Pull-ups -> Mobility -> Coffee -> Reading).
- Let users attach a reward ritual to a stack (e.g., coffee + reading).
- Add an identity or "why" field for each habit to reinforce motivation.
- Provide a quick morning cue to open the app and start the stack.
- Add a short end-of-stack reflection prompt to lock in satisfaction.
- Create nutrition idea prompts or a checklist for simple, repeatable wins.

### User story log (1% better, weekly)

- As a user, I want a pinned "Morning Stack" card with a Start button so my routine is front-and-center. (Obvious: top of home screen. Attractive: shows the coffee + reading reward. Easy: one click to begin. Satisfying: visible progress as each step checks off.)
- As a user, I want a simple reward ritual prompt after the last stack item so I transition to coffee + reading. (Obvious: appears at completion. Attractive: names the reward. Easy: no extra input. Satisfying: closes the loop.)
- As a user, I want a daily points progress bar at the top so I feel momentum early. (Obvious: fixed header. Attractive: highlights "points already banked." Easy: auto-calculated. Satisfying: quick win without extra work.)
- As a user, I want one-tap water logging in Nalgenes so hydration is effortless. (Obvious: water row has 1/2/3 buttons. Attractive: keeps the goal visible. Easy: no typing. Satisfying: clean tally and completion state.)
- As a user, I want a "minimum viable nutrition win" checklist so nutrition feels doable. (Obvious: appears under Nutrition. Attractive: clear, small targets. Easy: checkboxes. Satisfying: points earned without overthinking.)
- As a user, I want an identity note attached to each habit so I see the "practitioner" mindset. (Obvious: small label under the habit. Attractive: reinforces identity. Easy: short text field. Satisfying: daily confirmation.)
- As a user, I want a weekly "1% better" micro-goal so I keep compounding improvements. (Obvious: weekly goals panel. Attractive: one concise goal. Easy: single line. Satisfying: no breaking changes, just steady progress.)
